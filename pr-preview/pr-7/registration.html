Registration

Registration for the EarthCODE Hackathon 2026 will open in September 2026. Register here

Information for applicants

The EarthCODE Hackathon will be held from November 30 to December 4, 2026 at ESA ESRIN, Frascati, Italy. The event will provide an opportunity to bring the communities who are using EarthCODE tools and services as close as possible to the people who are building them.

We welcome applications from:

  • Earth system scientists working with Earth Observation (EO) data who want to create, reuse, or publish data collections.
  • Members of ESA Science Clusters, particularly addressing: Ocean, Carbon and Polar science communities.

We especially invite early career researchers, PhD students, and post-docs!

To best benefit from the event, we recommend some familiarity or experience with:

  • Earth Observation (EO) data (especially from Polar, Carbon, and Ocean Clusters, including interdisciplinary applications)
  • Programming skills using Python. We plan to accept participants with broad skill levels and backgrounds in programming. However, to best benefit from and contribute to the program, some experience with Python, R, or a similar programming language is recommended.
  • Familiarity with Git and GitHub would be beneficial, but not required. We will provide a short tutorial on Git and GitHub during the hackathon.

Please note that if there are more applicants than we have space, priority will be given to people who work with the ESA Polar Cluster, Ocean Cluster, Carbon Cluster, ScienceHub, Phi Lab, and people who work with ESA data.

Application

In the application form, we encourage you to think about and provide a science use case that you would like to prototype or work on. At the beginning of the hackathon, participants will be able to pitch their use case to support the formation of projects, and participants will form teams based on shared interests.

We are able to provide some needs-based travel support for participants who require it.

Applications will close on October 16, 2026. Accepted applicants will be notified no later than October 23, 2026. We expect to accommodate approximately 40-60 participants.
